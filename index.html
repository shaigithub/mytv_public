MyTV

A TV app for LG (webOS) and Samsung (Tizen) smart TVs.

MyTV plays your own IPTV playlist with a full programme guide, catch-up, and the broadcasters' free VOD. Each person in the household has a profile with their own favourites, lists and history.

Profile sync with a Google account (optional)

A profile can be linked to a Google account, so its favourites, lists, Continue watching, VOD progress, reminders and followed shows are the same on every TV where that account is linked. The data is kept in the account's own Google Drive, in a hidden folder that only MyTV can see. Linking is optional: a profile without an account stays on its TV.
