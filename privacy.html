MyTV privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

MyTV is a TV app for LG and Samsung smart TVs. It has no servers of its own and no user accounts. This policy explains what happens to your information, in particular when you link a profile to a Google account.

On the TV

Your settings, profiles, favourites, lists, watch history and VOD progress are stored on the TV itself. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else. The TV reads your playlist, guide and the broadcasters' sites directly.

Google account (optional)

You can link a profile to a Google account. MyTV then asks Google for two permissions, and nothing more:

  • Your email address (openid, email) — only to show on the TV which account the profile is linked to.
  • MyTV's own app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata) — a hidden folder that only MyTV can see. MyTV keeps one file there with the profile's favourites, lists, watch history and Continue watching, VOD progress and your shows, reminders, followed shows, alert settings, menu order and recent searches.

MyTV cannot see or change anything else in your Google account: not your other Drive files, Gmail, Photos or contacts. The data goes only between your TVs and your own Google Drive; the developer never receives it and has no access to it. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising or tracking.

After you allow it, the TV keeps a sign-in token so it can sync. The token stays on that TV: it is not included in backups or sent anywhere other than Google.

MyTV's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping and deleting

  • Unlink in MyTV (Settings → Profiles → Google account) stops syncing and revokes MyTV's access.
  • You can remove MyTV's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • To delete the synced file, open drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps → MyTV → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Deleting a profile on the TV, or uninstalling MyTV, removes its data from that TV.

Children

MyTV does not knowingly collect information from children; profiles for children are kept on the TV unless a parent links them to a Google account.

קישור פרופיל לחשבון Google הוא לא חובה. אם מקשרים, MyTV מבקשת רק את כתובת המייל (כדי להראות לאיזה חשבון הפרופיל מקושר) ואת תיקיית האפליקציה המוסתרת שלה ב-Google Drive, שבה נשמר קובץ אחד עם המועדפים, הרשימות, היסטוריית הצפייה וההתקדמות ב-VOD. MyTV לא רואה ולא משנה שום דבר אחר בחשבון. המידע עובר רק בין הטלוויזיות שלכם ל-Google Drive שלכם; המפתח לא מקבל אותו ואין לו גישה אליו. אפשר לנתק בכל רגע ב-MyTV (הגדרות ← פרופילים ← חשבון Google) או ב-myaccount.google.com/connections.
